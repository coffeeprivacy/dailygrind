Daily Grind

Privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026

The short version
  • Your drinks, photos, stamps and settings stay on your iPhone.
  • Friends uses your own iCloud account. Only people you invite, or whose invite you accept, see what you share.
  • Your Apple Health sleep data never leaves your phone.
  • The developer only receives what you choose to send: a support message or a report about a post.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and nothing is sold.

Who we are

Daily Grind is an iPhone app made by an independent developer ("we"). If you have a question about this policy, use Settings → Contact support in the app, or see the support page.

What stays on your iPhone

The drinks you log, their caffeine, notes, photos, the place you had them, your passport stamps, streaks, daily limit, bedtime, and the age, sex, pregnancy and weight details you give to work out your limit. These are stored only on your device. They're included in your iPhone's normal iCloud Backup if you have it turned on, which is handled by Apple, not us. We can't see any of it.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Daily Grind reads your sleep (including sleep stages) to compare it with your caffeine. It doesn't write anything to Apple Health. Sleep data is used only inside the app on your phone. It is never shared with friends, never sent to us, never stored in iCloud by the app, and never used for advertising or sold.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a drink. When a photo is used for a passport stamp, your phone checks it shows a drink and isn't a repeat. That check runs entirely on your phone.

Location

Location is used only when you tap the pin on a drink, to suggest nearby places. Exact coordinates stay on your phone. If you share that drink with friends, they see the place's name, not your location.

Friends (iCloud)

Friends works through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). When you use it, your display name, the drinks you choose to share from the last 30 days (with their photo, note and place name) and your reactions are stored in your own iCloud account and shared with the people you invite or whose invite you accept. We have no access to this data. Any drink can be kept private with its "Show to friends" switch, and shared drinks are removed after 30 days.

What the developer receives

We keep support messages for up to 12 months and reports for as long as needed to deal with them, then delete them.

What we don't do

No advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking, no data brokers, and no selling or renting of your information.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all my data removes everything on your phone and everything you've shared in iCloud. Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone. To have a support message or report deleted, ask us through Contact support.

Children

Daily Grind isn't aimed at children. For under-18s it suggests a lower caffeine limit based on published guidance. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK or EU GDPR, or the Australian Privacy Act), you can ask for a copy of any information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Contact us through the app and we'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
