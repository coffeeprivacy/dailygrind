Common questions

How is my daily limit worked out?

400 mg for adults (NHS, FDA, European Food Safety Authority), 200 mg if pregnant or breastfeeding (NHS, EFSA), and 2.5 mg per kg of body weight for under-18s (Health Canada). You can change it in Settings. It's general guidance, not medical advice.

Why isn't my sleep showing?

Daily Grind reads sleep from Apple Health. Check Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Daily Grind and allow Sleep. Garmin, COROS, Oura and other trackers need sleep sharing turned on in their own app first. New nights appear the next morning.

Why didn't my photo earn a passport stamp?

Stamps need a photo taken with the camera in Daily Grind, showing a drink, that you haven't used before, and the drink must keep you within your daily limit. You get one stamp per different drink.

How do I add a friend?

Open Friends, set your name and tap Invite a friend. When they open your link they see your shared drinks; send theirs back so you see each other's. Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud.

How do I block someone?

Tap ••• on one of their posts or next to their name and choose Block. You stop seeing each other's drinks straight away. You can unblock people in Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all my data removes everything on your phone and everything you've shared in iCloud.
